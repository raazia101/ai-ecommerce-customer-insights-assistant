# E-commerce Policies

## Return Policy

Eligible products can generally be returned within 30 days of delivery.

Products must be returned in acceptable condition and may need to include
their original packaging and accessories.

Some product categories may have different return conditions or may not be
eligible for return.

Customers should review the product-specific return information before
submitting a return request.

## Refund Policy

Refunds are issued after the returned product has been received and the
return has been approved.

Approved refunds are normally sent to the original payment method.

The time required for the refund to appear in a customer's account depends
on the payment provider or bank.

## Shipping Policy

Shipping times depend on the destination, selected delivery service, and
availability of the ordered products.

Estimated delivery information is shown during the ordering process and may
also appear in the customer's order details.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed and may change because of carrier
delays or other circumstances.

## Payment Policy

Supported payment methods are displayed during checkout.

A payment may be declined because of incorrect payment information,
insufficient funds, restrictions from the payment provider, or other
processing issues.

Customers should not repeatedly submit a failed payment if their bank or
payment provider indicates that the transaction requires attention.

## Account Security Policy

Customers are responsible for maintaining the security of their account
credentials.

Passwords should not be shared with other people.

If a customer believes that their account credentials have been
compromised, they should reset their password and contact customer support.

## Cancellation Policy

Cancellation requests should be submitted as soon as possible.

An order may not be cancellable once it has entered the shipping process.

If cancellation is no longer available, the customer may need to use the
return process after delivery if the product is eligible.
